# Slokamrita landing preview

Russian and English desktop designs for review, not the production app.

Catalog counts, the featured verse and editorial notes are static.
Verse links, the public recitation and book collection links use Slokamrita.
The three sample community invitations have no destination.

Brand assets and project photographs come from Slokamrita and Sri Rupa Seva Kunj.
The journal artwork is an illustration based on a single photograph of the
garden kutir, not a photograph or a map. Product screenshots are from
Slokamrita and use public verse content or isolated demo data.
