# Slokamrita landing preview

Russian and English desktop designs for review, not the production app.

Catalog counts, the featured verse and the personal-entry example are static.
Verse links, the public recitation and book collection links use Slokamrita.
The three sample community invitations have no destination.

Brand assets and project photographs come from the Sri Rupa Seva Kunj projects.
The Vedarama thumbnail is from its approved reader mockup. The Bhajanamrita
thumbnail is from its public catalog. The Vrajapedia photo is from its
[Sri Radha-Gokulananda article](https://vrajapedia.com/en/places/radha-gokulananda).
